## Program Objectives

Development of scramjet propulsion technology for hypersonic flight applications, supporting both defense programs and future high-speed commercial transport research.

### Key Deliverables
1. Scramjet engine prototype (Mach 5-8)
2. Thermal protection materials
3. Fuel injection system
4. Ground test validation
5. Flight test article
