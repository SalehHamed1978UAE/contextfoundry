## Segment Commentary

### Nexus Aerospace ($765M, +8.5%)
- Strong commercial aviation deliveries, particularly 787 and A350 components
- Falcon X UAV program hit Critical Design Review milestone
- Orbital Satellite Constellation on track for first launch in Q3 2026
- Defense orders increased with new F-35 component contract

### Nexus Energy Systems ($668M, +14.8%)
- Desert Sun Solar Farm construction progressing ahead of schedule
- GreenHydrogen Initiative completed Front-End Engineering
- SmartGrid Controller secured two new utility pilots
- Record renewable energy project pipeline of $4.2B

### Nexus Digital Solutions ($512M, +20.5%)
- NexusConnect IoT platform added 52 new enterprise customers
- CyberShield Security Platform completed successful beta program
- QuantumLeap Computing secured DARPA research grant
- Professional services utilization reached 85%

### Nexus Advanced Materials ($355M, -3.5%)
- Aerospace composites demand temporarily softened
- AeroMaterials program completed material formulation phase
- BatteryTech Solid State achieved lab cell demonstration
- HTS Wire program achieved 1 km continuous wire milestone
