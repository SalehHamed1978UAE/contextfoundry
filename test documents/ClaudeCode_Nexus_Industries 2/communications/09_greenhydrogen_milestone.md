## 🎉 WE DID IT! 🎉

Today marks a historic moment for Nexus Industries and our Energy Systems Division. At 10:47 AM CDT, our GreenHydrogen facility in El Paso, Texas, produced its first molecules of green hydrogen!

---

## Milestone Achievement

| Achievement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| First Production | March 20, 2026, 10:47 AM CDT |
| Initial Rate | 85 kg H₂/hour |
| Purity | 99.9997% (exceeds specification) |
| Facility Status | Operational |

GreenHydrogen represents the future of clean energy:

- **Jobs:** 45 permanent positions, 200+ construction jobs
- **Investment:** Largest clean hydrogen facility in the Southwest
