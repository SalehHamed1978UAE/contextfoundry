# FIN-006: Q3 2024 Quarterly Report

**MedSync Health**
**Date:** October 15, 2024
**Currency:** United Arab Emirates Dirham (AED)

---

## 1. Executive Summary

MedSync Health delivered a strong performance in the third quarter of 2024, continuing its trajectory of rapid growth as it prepares for its initial public offering (IPO). Total revenue for Q3 2024 reached **AED 124,950,000**, representing a 30.8% increase over Q3 2023 (AED 95,550,000) and a 88.9% increase over Q3 2022 (AED 66,150,000).

The quarter was marked by the successful integration of the Series D funding round (AED 551,250,000 in June 2024), which fueled strategic investments in R&D and Sales & Marketing. The Gross Margin remained robust at **75.0%**, consistent with the full-year forecast. Net loss for the quarter was contained, reflecting planned high growth-stage operating expenses.
