## Notes for System Testing

1. **Ambiguous Questions:** Some questions (e.g., "What is the retention rate?") intentionally have multiple valid answers to test disambiguation capabilities.

2. **Calculation Questions:** Multi-hop questions often require mathematical operations on values from one or more documents.

3. **Unanswerable Questions:** The 15 questions marked [NOT IN DOCUMENTS] test the system's ability to recognize when information is not available.

4. **Cross-Document Questions:** Many questions require information from multiple documents to answer correctly.

5. **Temporal Reasoning:** Temporal questions test the system's ability to understand time relationships and calculate growth rates.

6. **Policy Variations:** Policy questions often have level-based or role-based variations that require careful reading.
