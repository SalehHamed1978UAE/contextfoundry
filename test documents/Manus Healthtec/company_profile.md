# Company Profile: MedSync Health

## Company Overview

**Company Name:** MedSync Health, Inc.

**Industry:** Healthcare Technology

**Founded:** 2018

**Headquarters:** Boston, Massachusetts, USA

**European Operations:** London, UK (established 2022)

**Stage:** Series C (raised $85M in Series C, December 2024)

**Total Funding:** $165M

**Employees:** 487 (as of December 2025)

## Mission Statement

MedSync Health delivers intelligent care coordination platforms that connect hospitals, clinics, and patients through real-time data integration and AI-powered clinical workflows.

## Product Overview

**Primary Product:** MedSync Platform - A cloud-based care coordination system that integrates with EHR systems (Epic, Cerner, Allscripts) to streamline patient transitions, reduce readmissions, and improve care team communication.

**Key Features:**
- Real-time patient status tracking
- AI-powered discharge planning
- Automated care team notifications
- HIPAA-compliant messaging
- Predictive readmission risk scoring
- Integration with 15+ major EHR systems

## Market Position

- **Target Market:** Mid-to-large hospitals (200+ beds) and health systems
- **Current Customer Base:** 142 hospital clients across 38 US states
- **Market Share:** 8.3% of US care coordination software market (2025)
