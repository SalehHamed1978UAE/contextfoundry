# User Research Summary - Q4 2025

**Conducted by:** Product Team
**Date:** January 5, 2026

## Research Objectives

- Understand how customers are using the MedSync Platform
- Identify pain points and areas for improvement
- Gather feedback on the upcoming Analytics Suite

## Methodology

- Conducted 15 customer interviews
- Surveyed 50 users

## Key Findings

1. **High Satisfaction with Core Platform:** Users are very satisfied with the core care coordination features.
2. **Need for Better Analytics:** Users want more robust analytics and reporting capabilities.
3. **UI Could Be Modernized:** Some users feel that the UI is starting to look dated.
4. **Strong Interest in Analytics Suite:** Users are excited about the upcoming Analytics Suite and the predictive readmission risk scoring feature.

## Recommendations

- Prioritize the Analytics Suite launch.
- Begin planning for a UI/UX refresh.
- Continue to invest in customer research to stay close to user needs.
