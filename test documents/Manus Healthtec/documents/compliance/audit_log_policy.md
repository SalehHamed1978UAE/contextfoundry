# Audit Log Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines the requirements for audit logging across all MedSync Health systems.

## Scope

All systems that process customer data, including the MedSync Platform, databases, and internal tools.

## Requirements

- **What to Log:** User authentication, data access, data modifications, system configuration changes.
- **Log Retention:** Logs must be retained for at least 1 year.
- **Log Security:** Logs must be stored securely and protected from unauthorized access or modification.
- **Log Monitoring:** Logs must be monitored for suspicious activity.

## Compliance

This policy supports our SOC 2, HIPAA, and ISO 27001 compliance requirements.
