# MedSync Health, Inc.
# Data Retention Policy

**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026

## Purpose

This policy defines how long MedSync Health retains different types of data and the procedures for data deletion.

## Retention Periods

| Data Type | Retention Period |
|---|---|
| Customer Data (PHI) | Duration of contract + 7 years |
| Financial Records | 7 years |
| Employee Records | 7 years after termination |
| System Logs | 1 year |
| Email | 3 years |

## Data Deletion

- Data will be securely deleted at the end of the retention period.
- Customers can request deletion of their data upon contract termination, subject to legal and regulatory requirements.

## Legal Holds

- Data subject to legal holds will be retained until the hold is lifted, regardless of the retention period.
