## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy establishes the official organizational structure and defines the process for managing and updating departmental headcounts within MedSync Health. The organizational chart (Org Chart) serves as the definitive visual representation of reporting relationships, functional divisions, and the allocation of human resources across the company. This document ensures clarity, accountability, and consistency in the company's operational framework.

The structure is designed to support MedSync Health's strategic goals as a Series D healthcare technology company [1], ensuring optimal resource deployment across its four core product lines [2].

## 2. Organizational Structure Policy

### 2.1. Structure Overview

MedSync Health operates under a **Functional Organizational Structure**, where employees are grouped based on their specialized roles (e.g., Engineering, Marketing). All functional departments report directly to the Chief Executive Officer (CEO) or a designated C-level executive, ensuring clear lines of authority and centralized strategic direction.

## 4. Policy on Organizational Change and Headcount Management

### 4.1. Headcount Approval Process

Any permanent change to the authorized headcount (increase or decrease) must follow a strict approval process to maintain financial discipline and strategic alignment.

1.  **Request Initiation:** Department VP/Head submits a formal Headcount Change Request (HCR) to the CHRO and CFO.
2.  **Review and Justification:** The HCR must include a detailed business case, budget impact analysis (referencing the department's FY2024 budget [4]), and a clear definition of the role's level (IC, Manager, Director, VP).
3.  **Approval Thresholds:**
    *   **IC and Manager Roles:** Requires approval from the Department VP/Head, CHRO, and CFO.
    *   **Director Roles:** Requires approval from the Department VP/Head, CHRO, CFO, and COO.
    *   **VP and C-Level Roles:** Requires unanimous approval from the Executive Leadership Team and final sign-off by the CEO.
4.  **Policy Exception:** Any deviation from the established headcount levels or organizational structure requires explicit, written approval from the CEO.

### 4.2. Level-Based Variations

Job titles and levels (Individual Contributor, Manager, Director, Vice President) are standardized across MedSync Health to ensure equitable compensation and career progression.

*   **Individual Contributor (IC):** Focuses on execution and specialized tasks. Average compensation is **AED 294,000** [5].
*   **Manager:** Responsible for day-to-day team management and performance. Average compensation is **AED 441,000** [5].
*   **Director:** Responsible for a significant functional area or program. Average compensation is **AED 661,500** [5].
*   **Vice President (VP):** Responsible for an entire department or major business unit. Average compensation is **AED 1,102,500** [5].

*See HR-003: Job Titles and Compensation Framework for full definitions and eligibility criteria.*
