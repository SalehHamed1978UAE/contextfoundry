# STRAT-004: Company OKRs Q4 2024

**MedSync Health**
**Document ID:** STRAT-004
**Date:** October 1, 2024
**Version:** 1.0
**Author:** Strategy & Planning Office

## 1. Executive Summary

This document outlines the high-level Objectives and Key Results (OKRs) for MedSync Health for the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2024 (Q4 2024). As the final quarter of the fiscal year, the primary focus is on achieving the annual financial and operational targets, ensuring a strong foundation for the upcoming Series E funding round, and maintaining high customer satisfaction as we scale.

These company-level OKRs are designed to align with the overall FY 2024 strategic plan and are cascaded down to departmental and individual OKRs (e.g., see STRAT-005 for Engineering OKRs). All financial targets are based on the master data model (see FIN-001: FY 2024 Financial Plan for detailed breakdown).

## 2. Q4 2024 Company Objectives and Key Results

### Objective 1: Achieve FY2024 Financial Targets and Secure Capital for Next Phase

**Goal:** Successfully close the fiscal year by meeting all revenue and efficiency targets, and prepare the financial and strategic groundwork for the Series E funding round.
