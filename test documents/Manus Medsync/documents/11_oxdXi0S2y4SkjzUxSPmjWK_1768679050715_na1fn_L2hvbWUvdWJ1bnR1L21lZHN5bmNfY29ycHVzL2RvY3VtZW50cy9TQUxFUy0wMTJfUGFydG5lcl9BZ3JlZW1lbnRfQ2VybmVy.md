## 1. Executive Summary

This Strategic Partner Agreement (the "Agreement") outlines the framework for a deep, mutually beneficial partnership between **MedSync Health** and **Cerner Corporation**. The primary goal is to integrate MedSync Health's cutting-edge digital health platform, particularly **MedSync Connect** (PROD-CONNECT) and **MedSync Analytics** (PROD-ANALYTICS), directly into the Cerner Millennium Electronic Health Record (EHR) ecosystem. This collaboration will enhance clinical workflows, improve data interoperability, and deliver superior patient outcomes for our joint customers.

MedSync Health, a Series D healthcare technology company founded in January 2019, has established itself as a leader in healthcare data integration and patient engagement. Our platform is currently utilized by **520 Hospitals** and **3,200 Clinics** globally, demonstrating a robust market presence and proven value proposition.

## 3. Scope of Partnership

The partnership will focus on three core areas:

### 3.1. Technical Integration

*   **MedSync Connect Integration:** Seamless, bi-directional data exchange between MedSync Connect and Cerner Millennium via FHIR APIs. This integration was initially explored in April 2023 (See **LEGAL-005: Cerner Integration MOU**).
*   **MedSync Analytics Data Feed:** Provision of aggregated, de-identified data from Cerner systems to MedSync Analytics for enhanced reporting and predictive modeling.
*   **Single Sign-On (SSO):** Implementation of SSO for joint customers to ensure a frictionless user experience for clinicians.

### 3.2. Co-Selling and Go-to-Market

*   **Joint Sales Strategy:** Development of a unified sales motion targeting health systems currently using Cerner Millennium.
*   **Referral Program:** Establishment of a formal referral and commission structure for leads generated by both parties.
*   **Marketing Collaboration:** Joint case studies, webinars, and presence at industry events (e.g., HIMSS, ViVE).

### 3.3. Product Focus and Cross-Reference

The initial focus will be on the following MedSync Health products:

## 4. Financial Terms and Revenue Sharing

The financial model is based on a tiered revenue-sharing structure for all new joint customers.

### 4.1. Revenue Distribution (FY 2026 Projections)

| Tier | Joint Annual Contract Value (ACV) | Cerner Share | MedSync Health Share |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1** | < AED 1,000,000 | 15% | 85% |
| **Tier 2** | AED 1,000,000 - 5,000,000 | 20% | 80% |
| **Tier 3** | > AED 5,000,000 | 25% | 75% |

### 4.2. Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) Efficiency

MedSync Health's current Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) is **AED 152,970** per new customer. This partnership is projected to reduce the effective CAC for joint deals by 30% due to Cerner's established sales channels and trust with health systems.

Our strong LTV:CAC Ratio of **10.7** provides a solid foundation for investing in this strategic channel.
