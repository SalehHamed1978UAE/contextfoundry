**MedSync Health**

## 1. Executive Summary

MedSync Health continued its strong growth trajectory in the third quarter of fiscal year 2023, driven by the successful integration of our product suite and expansion in the North American and EMEA markets. Total revenue for Q3 2023 reached **AED 95,550,000**, representing a significant 44.44% year-over-year growth compared to Q3 2022. This performance was supported by a robust Net Revenue Retention (NRR) rate of 118% (as per the latest available full-year projection), demonstrating the strong value proposition of our platform and successful upselling within the existing customer base.

The company's focus on compliance and security remains paramount. For a detailed review of our security and regulatory posture, including our SOC 2 Type II and HIPAA certifications, please **See SEC-001 for compliance details**.
