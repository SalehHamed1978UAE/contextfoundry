## ADR-XXX: Piece 0.6 governed disposition of foundational relations

Date: 2026-05

Decision:
`HOLDS_POSITION`, `WORKS_AT`, and `REPORTS_TO` are governed as `core` relations. Both existing `HOLDS_POSITION` signature rows are preserved and adopted into `core`: `PERSON → JOB_TITLE` and `PERSON → ORGANIZATION`. `WORKS_AT PERSON → ORGANIZATION` and `REPORTS_TO PERSON → PERSON` are also adopted into `core`.

`HAS_COMPENSATION PERSON → COMPENSATION` is governed as a `finance` relation. The older `HAS_COMPENSATION PERSON → CONCEPT` row is deprecated, preserved by UUID, and not deleted.

No sibling relations are changed in Piece 0.6. `HELD_POSITION`, `AFFILIATED_WITH`, `MANAGES`, `OWNS`, and other sibling or near-duplicate relations remain future governance scope unless separately approved.

Rationale:
Piece 2 scoped prompts use `core ∪ primary_domain`. If required organizational relations remain `domain_id = NULL`, they are excluded from scoped prompts and domain-aware extraction loses foundational organizational facts. The Piece 0.6 audit showed that both `HOLDS_POSITION` rows are valid signature overloads and actively used, `WORKS_AT` is the canonical employment relation expected by code paths, and `REPORTS_TO` is cross-domain organizational structure. The audit also showed that the canonical compensation row is `PERSON → COMPENSATION`, while the older `PERSON → CONCEPT` row is unused and mis-targeted.

Implication:
Piece 2 can build scoped prompts without losing organizational role, employment, and reporting relations. Compensation extraction remains finance-scoped. HR-domain compensation extraction remains recorded under Gap 6 and is not solved by this decision.
