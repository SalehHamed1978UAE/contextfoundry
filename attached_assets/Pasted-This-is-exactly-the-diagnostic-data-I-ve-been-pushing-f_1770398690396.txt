## The latency problem is a shipping blocker you haven't planned for

Tree retrieval taking 5-10 minutes on some queries isn't a performance optimization issue — it's a product viability issue. Your plan doesn't mention latency at all. The RLM circuit breaker tripping repeatedly means the tree traversal is hitting pathological cases (probably high-degree nodes or circular paths in the graph).

You have two options and you need to pick one before building the conflict-aware components on top of tree retrieval:

**Option A: Query timeout with graceful degradation.** Set a hard 30-second timeout on tree retrieval. If it doesn't return in 30 seconds, fall back to semantic search. This aligns with your existing routing architecture — it's just another signal (timeout_triggered → doc fallback). The 4 questions tree retrieval fixes (Q2, Q61, Q75, Q100) all likely resolve in under 30 seconds; the pathological cases (Q46, Q52, Q55) are the ones hanging.

**Option B: Precompute common traversal paths.** For the relationship patterns tree retrieval is good at (person → role, entity → specification), precompute the subgraphs at ingestion time and serve them as cached lookups. This eliminates the latency problem entirely for the patterns that matter, at the cost of staleness when the KG updates.

Option A is a one-hour fix. Option B is a day of work but produces a better system. I'd do Option A now and Option B when you have time.
