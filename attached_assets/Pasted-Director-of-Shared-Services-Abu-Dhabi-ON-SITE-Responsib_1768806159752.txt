Director of Shared Services

ON SITE
Responsibilities

Lead the overall program plan, governance, and milestones for the setup of shared services in coordination with the JOC.
Manage cross-functional workstreams including Procurement, HR, and IT.
Lead the requirement gathering and validation process across all subsidiaries to define business, functional, and governance requirements.
Serve as the main point of contact between us and the JOC.
Contracting of JV.
Work with legal, finance, and strategy to ensure that the Master Services Agreement establishes clear performance, cost, and accountability structures.
Act as business lead providing input on service scope, KPIs, and governance clauses.
Ensure that the Operating Agreement defines transparent decision rights, escalation, and other critical mechanisms.
Co-design and validate the shared services model.
Define business and functional requirements for each service area, working with relevant function leaders.
Translate business requirements into service design inputs, ensuring these are accurately captured by JOC.
Challenge and validate JOC process designs, technology stack, service catalogs, and performance metrics.
Define future-state processes and digital enablers aligned with best practices.
Ensure interoperability with existing ERP and digital platforms, both current and future.


Governance and Risk Management

Develop and detail the Shared Services Governance Framework, including decision rights, SLAs, escalation protocols, and reporting cadence.
Set up performance dashboards, risk registers, and compliance mechanisms.
Support performance management and value realization frameworks.


Capability Building

Help define key capabilities required from our side.
Define our internal organization structure to sustain service oversight post-implementation, including service owners, vendor management, and analytics.
Transfer knowledge to permanent staff and help build governance maturity.


Innovation and Future Enablement

Identify AI and automation use cases within shared services, such as generative AI for procurement analytics, predictive finance reporting, and digital assistants for HR queries.
Work with provider and internal IT/Digital teams to create a roadmap for progressive automation.
