### `docs/decisions.md` ADR

Append a new ADR using the next available number:

> ### ADR-XXX (2026-05): Piece 0.6 governed disposition of foundational relations
>
> **Decision:** `HOLDS_POSITION`, `WORKS_AT`, and `REPORTS_TO` are governed as `core` relations. `HAS_COMPENSATION` (PERSON → COMPENSATION, the v1.0.0 canonical row) is governed as `finance`. `HAS_COMPENSATION` (PERSON → CONCEPT, the v0.0.1 mis-targeted row) is deprecated. All UUIDs preserved.
>
> **Rationale:** Piece 2 scoped prompts use `core ∪ primary_domain`. Without this disposition, organizational role/reports/employment extraction would silently disappear under domain-scoped prompts. The canonical compensation relation belongs to finance per Piece 2.0 design §4. The PERSON → CONCEPT compensation row is mis-targeted (CONCEPT is a generic catch-all type), has zero usage in `relationships`, and is replaced by the v1.0.0 PERSON → COMPENSATION row.
>
> **Implication:** Piece 2 can build scoped prompts without losing organizational role-resolution edges. Compensation extraction remains finance-scoped; HR-domain compensation extraction remains an open architecture-level gap (Gap 6) to be addressed via secondary_domains support, document_type overlays, or an explicit HR domain — none of which are in Piece 0.6 scope.
