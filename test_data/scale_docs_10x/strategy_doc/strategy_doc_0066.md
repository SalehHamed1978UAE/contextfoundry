## Executive Summary

This document outlines the strategic approach to microservices refactoring for the upcoming quarter. Key stakeholders include Frontend Team and Backend Team.

## Background

The need for microservices refactoring has become increasingly apparent as Analytics Service faces resource exhaustion. Mia White initially raised this concern in Q2.

## Proposed Approach

- The team discussed the impact of microservices refactoring on Shipping Service.
- According to Parker Harris, we need to address technical debt before proceeding with microservices refactoring.
- Mia White suggested involving DevOps Team in the microservices refactoring initiative.
- The team discussed the impact of microservices refactoring on SMS Gateway.
