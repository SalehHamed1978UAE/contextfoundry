## Executive Summary

This document outlines the strategic approach to team restructuring for the upcoming quarter. Key stakeholders include Frontend Team and SRE Team.

The need for team restructuring has become increasingly apparent as Inventory Service faces error rates increasing. Logan Jackson initially raised this concern in Q1.
