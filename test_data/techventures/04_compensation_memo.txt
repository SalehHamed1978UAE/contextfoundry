COMPENSATION PHILOSOPHY
================================================================================

Our compensation philosophy targets the 75th percentile of comparable venture 
capital firms and technology companies. Total compensation includes:
- Competitive base salary
- Performance-based annual bonus
- Long-term equity incentives
- Comprehensive benefits package

Bonus targets are tied to:
- Fund performance (40%)
- Portfolio company outcomes (30%)
- Individual objectives (30%)
