# Capacity Planning Strategy

**Author:** Taylor Kim
**Last Updated:** 2025-08-25
**Status:** Draft

## Executive Summary

This document outlines the strategic approach to capacity planning for the upcoming quarter. Key stakeholders include QA Team and Growth Team.

## Background

The need for capacity planning has become increasingly apparent as Shipping Service faces timeout errors. Taylor Kim initially raised this concern in Q2.

## Proposed Approach

- According to Emerson Wilson, we need to address resource exhaustion before proceeding with capacity planning.
- The team discussed the impact of capacity planning on Search Service.
- Taylor Kim proposed that we should prioritize capacity planning before Q4.
- The team discussed the impact of capacity planning on Payment Service.
- Sage Robinson recommended a proof-of-concept for capacity planning using SMS Gateway.

## Timeline

- Phase 1 (Week 1-2): Planning and requirements
- Phase 2 (Week 3-6): Implementation
- Phase 3 (Week 7-8): Testing and rollout

## Risks and Mitigations

- scaling bottlenecks may impact timeline
- Resource constraints in Infrastructure Team
- Dependencies on SMS Gateway
