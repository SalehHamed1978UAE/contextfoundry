## Executive Summary

This document outlines the strategic approach to budget allocation for the upcoming quarter. Key stakeholders include SRE Team and QA Team.

## Background

The need for budget allocation has become increasingly apparent as Analytics Service faces resource exhaustion. Finley Moore initially raised this concern in Q3.

- Morgan Chen suggested involving Data Team in the budget allocation initiative.

- Kendall Thomas suggested involving Growth Team in the budget allocation initiative.
- There was significant debate about budget allocation. Kendall Thomas advocated for a phased approach.
