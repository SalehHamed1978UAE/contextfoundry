# TechCorp Company Overview

TechCorp was founded in 2015 by Jane Smith.
The company focuses on AI solutions for enterprise customers.

## Leadership Team

- Jane Smith - CEO and Founder (2015-present)
- Bob Johnson - CTO (2016-present)  
- Alice Chen - CFO (2018-present)
- David Park - VP Engineering (2019-2022)
- Sarah Lee - VP Engineering (2022-present)
- Michael Wong - Head of Sales (2020-present)

## Funding History

- Seed Round: $1M (2015) - led by Angel Investors
- Series A: $5M (2016) - led by Venture Capital Fund
- Series B: $20M (2018) - led by Growth Partners
- Series C: $50M (2021) - led by Mega Investments

## Products

- TechCorp AI Platform - flagship enterprise AI product
- TechCorp Analytics - data analytics suite
- TechCorp Assistant - AI-powered customer service
