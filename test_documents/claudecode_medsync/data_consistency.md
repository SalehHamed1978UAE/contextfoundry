## Ambiguity Cases (Intentional)

### Case 1: Retention Metrics
- Customer retention rate: 93.8% (100% - 6.2% logo churn)
- Logo retention: 93.8%
- Net Revenue Retention (NRR): 124%
- Gross Revenue Retention (GRR): 94%

Question "What is the retention rate?" is ambiguous.

### Case 2: OKR Target Format
Document STRAT-005 will contain:
"KR2: Acquire 180 new customers (Target: 150, Stretch: 220)"
- 180 is the committed KR
- 150 is labeled "Target"
- 220 is "Stretch"

### Case 3: Level-Based Policies
Referral bonus varies by level ($2,000 to $15,000).
PTO varies by level (20-25 days or unlimited).

### Case 4: Product Revenue Ambiguity
- MedSync Core revenue: $78.4M
- But "Hospital segment" revenue could include some MedSync Connect hospital customers
- "Enterprise revenue" ($98 customers × avg) ≠ MedSync Core Enterprise tier

### Case 5: Employee Count Timing
- 1,200 as of Dec 31, 2024
- Target 1,500 by Dec 31, 2025
- Q1 2025 count will be ~1,250

### Case 6: Fiscal vs Calendar Year
All references use calendar year, but some industry reports use fiscal years.
FY 2024 = Jan 1 - Dec 31, 2024 (not offset)

### Case 7: ACV vs ARR
- ARR (Annual Recurring Revenue): $168.2M
- Total customers: 589
- Implied ACV: $285,568
- But this includes varying contract lengths

### Case 8: Geographic Revenue
- EMEA revenue: $32.9M (21%)
- But London + Berlin employees are 330 (27.5% of headcount)
- Revenue per employee varies by region
